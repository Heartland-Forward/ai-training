How to get access

Who qualifies, the steps, and the fine print for each tool.

Claude for TeachersFree through July 2028 · sign up yourself

Anthropic's free Claude plan for US K-12 educators, with higher limits than a free account.

Who qualifies

US K-12 teachers, instructional coaches, specialists, librarians, counselors, and other certificated staff. Students can't use it.

Steps

  1. Sign in to Claude with your school email, or make a new account with it. A personal-email account won't work.
  2. Go to claude.ai/teachers and click Get started.
  3. Accept the K-12 terms and pick your school.
  4. Verify your role. You may need to upload a school ID, contract, or pay stub, and review can take up to 3 days.

Good to know

  • Free through July 30, 2028.
  • Anthropic doesn't train its models on your chats.
  • Your school or district can also set it up for all staff at no cost.

ChatGPT for TeachersFree through June 2028 · sign up yourself

OpenAI's free ChatGPT workspace for US K-12 educators.

Who qualifies

Teachers, staff, school leaders, and district administrators at an accredited US K-12 school or district.

Steps

  1. Go to chatgpt.com/k12-verification and sign in with your school email.
  2. Verify your role through SheerID, OpenAI's verification partner.
  3. Join or create your district's workspace, then set your role, subjects, and grades.

Good to know

  • Free through June 2028. OpenAI says it will give notice before any price change.
  • It's a separate product from ChatGPT Plus and Pro.

Khanmigo for TeachersFree teacher tools · sign up yourself

Khan Academy's free AI tools for lesson planning and class prep.

Who qualifies

Adults who work for a school as a teacher or other staff. You confirm this yourself when you accept the terms.

Steps

  1. Go to Khanmigo teacher tools and click Sign up for free.
  2. Create or log in to a Khan Academy account with its language set to English.
  3. Fill in your details and accept the terms.

Good to know

  • Free, with no end date announced.
  • Students can only get Khanmigo through a school or district plan.

Canva EducationFree · teaching roles only

Free premium Canva for K-12 teaching staff.

Who qualifies

Certified K-12 teachers, school librarians, learning support staff, and curriculum specialists. Counselors and advisors aren't on Canva's list, so ask whether your school already has Canva for all staff.

Steps

  1. Go to canva.com/edu-signup and sign up with your school email, or your school's Google, Microsoft, or Clever account.
  2. Choose Teacher and enter your school details.
  3. If Canva doesn't recognize your school email, upload your teaching license. Canva reviews it within 48 hours.

Good to know

  • Free while you stay eligible.
  • Canva asks you to re-verify every 3 years.

Microsoft 365 Copilot ChatIncluded with some school Microsoft accounts

Microsoft's AI chat, included with some schools' Microsoft 365 accounts.

Who qualifies

Staff at schools with a Microsoft 365 A1, A3, or A5 license. Students 13 and up get it only if the school turns it on.

Steps

  1. Go to m365copilot.com and sign in with your school email.
  2. Look for the green shield at the top of the chat. It means you're in your school account.
  3. No sign-in or no shield? Your school may not have it turned on. Ask whoever set up your school email.

Good to know

  • Free with your school's license.
  • The full Microsoft 365 Copilot, inside Word and Outlook, is a paid add-on for schools ($18 per user per month).

Gemini for EducationIncluded with some school Google accounts

Google's AI chat, included with Google Workspace for Education accounts.

Who qualifies

Users 18 and over on a school Google account, unless the school turns it off.

Steps

  1. Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with your school email.
  2. Check that the account in the top-right corner is your school one.
  3. Won't open? Your school may have it turned off. Ask whoever set up your school email.

Good to know

  • Free with your school's Google account.
  • Gemini in Google Classroom follows the same rule.
